How to install

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. When it finishes, tap the download notification (or open it from your Files app).
  3. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.
How do I update?

Download and install the new version from this page. It replaces the old one and keeps your data.

Why does Android warn me?

Android shows these prompts for any app installed from outside the Play Store. That's expected for this app.
